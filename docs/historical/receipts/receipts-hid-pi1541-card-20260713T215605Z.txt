Compatibility assessment
------------------------

Official Raspberry Pi documentation states that the normal kernel filename for Raspberry Pi 2, 3, 3+, and Zero 2 W is kernel7.img. In 64-bit mode, Zero 2 W uses an appropriate 64-bit kernel such as kernel8.img. This card supplies neither filename and does not explicitly direct firmware to kernel.img.

Source:

- https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/computers/config_txt.html#kernel

The observed Zero 2 boot failure is therefore expected from this card's contents and provides no new evidence about the Zero 2 WH hardware.

Conclusion
----------

Do not use this 480 MiB PI1541 card as the second Raspberry Pi OS control. It is too small for the 2.8 GB Raspberry Pi OS image and lacks the Zero 2 W kernel and device tree. A valid second-card control requires a known-good card of at least 4 GB, preferably 8 GB or larger.
